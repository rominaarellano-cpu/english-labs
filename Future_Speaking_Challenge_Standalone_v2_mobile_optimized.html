Quick Future Support

WILL

Prediction · spontaneous decision · offer · promise
+
subject + will + verb
I think it will rain.
−
subject + won’t + verb
I won’t stay home.
?
Will + subject + verb?
Will you help me?

GOING TO

Plan · intention · prediction with evidence now
+
subject + am/is/are going to + verb
I’m going to travel.
−
subject + am/is/are not going to + verb
I’m not going to drive.
?
Am/Is/Are + subject + going to + verb?
Are you going to travel?

PRESENT CONTINUOUS

Confirmed future arrangement
+
subject + am/is/are + verb-ing
I’m meeting Ana tomorrow.
−
subject + am/is/are not + verb-ing
I’m not working tomorrow.
?
Am/Is/Are + subject + verb-ing?
Are you working tomorrow?
