Class 2 · Future forms
WEEK 4RETRIEVECONNECTINTEGRATEREHEARSE

Prediction, plan or arrangement?

Review the three future meanings and their affirmative, negative and interrogative forms.

PREDICTION · WILL

WILL

+ I think it will rain.

− The tour won't be busy.

? Will it rain tomorrow?

PLAN · GOING TO

GOING TO

+ We are going to visit Chiloé.

− We aren't going to rent a car.

? Are you going to visit the market?

ARRANGEMENT · PRESENT CONTINUOUS

PRESENT CONTINUOUS

+ We are meeting the guide at 9.

− We aren't staying tonight.

? Are you taking the 8:00 bus?

Apoyo: Distingue el significado antes de elegir la forma: will = predicción · going to = plan/intención · present continuous = acuerdo o actividad ya coordinada. Revisa también las formas afirmativa, negativa e interrogativa.

SITUATION 1 · COAST

Friends in Bahía Inglesa

SITUATION 2 · CULTURE

Older adults in Frutillar

Two older adults want a cultural experience. They plan to visit the lake area tomorrow and already have tickets for a concert at 6:00 PM.

Use what the situation needs: traveller + tourism experience + destination/activity + reason + can/should + will / going to / present continuous
Example · Situation 1
I recommend a beach experience in Bahía Inglesa because it is a good option for a group of friends. You can spend the morning at the beach. You are meeting the guide at 10:00, and I think it will be very windy in the afternoon, so you should bring a light jacket.
Instrucción: Resuelve las dos situaciones. Para cada una, usa únicamente los elementos que la situación necesita: traveller + tourism experience + destination/activity + reason + can/should + una forma de futuro pertinente. El ejemplo de la Situación 1 es opcional.
